🖨️ Print your hang cards

  1. Print this page at 100% scale ("Actual size" — not "Fit to page").
  2. In the print dialog, turn ON "Background graphics" so the cards print dark.
  3. Use cardstock if you have it. Regular paper works fine too — it's a hang group, not a law firm.
  4. Cut along the dashed lines. 10 cards per sheet.
  5. Walk around the park. See a cool dude. Hand him a card. Done.
